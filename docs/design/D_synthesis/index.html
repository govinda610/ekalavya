Onboarding · The Threshold

Droṇa turned him away at the door.

Ekalavya came to the greatest teacher of the age and was refused — not for want of skill, but for being a forest-born outsider, the wrong caste, from outside the hall.

So he did not beg. He went into the forest, shaped Droṇa's likeness from clay, and set to work alone — until the day the royal archers found that the boy no one would teach had surpassed them all.

This tool is built for that boy. For the economics grad locked out of the CS program. For the career-changer, the self-taught, the one learning it in the forest because the hall was closed. Draw the string.
