Ekalavya

एकलव्य · स्वाध्याय

The self-taught outsider who mastered archery alone in the forest, before a clay statue of the guru who refused him. An AI coding tutor rendered as a Pahari illuminated manuscript — gold leaf, indigo night, and devotion over privilege.

A single-file design playground / style guide. Everything below is inline SVG + CSS — no chrome emoji, no neon, no gradient wordmark. This is a review artifact; it does not touch the real app.

01

Palette & Rationale

Warm, earthy pigments ground the manuscript. Indigo night is the folio; gold leaf is precious — reserved for mastery, earned progress, and primary CTAs. Vermilion is loss/danger only. Peacock teal signals information and freshly-unlocked skill.
